# Social icons

Brand marks, kept apart from the rest of `icons/` because their shapes aren't
ours to redraw: a network's mark has to be the official one, or not used at all.

Each is stripped to a bare `<svg>` with `fill="currentColor"` so it takes its
colour from the button around it, and its `<title>` removed so screen readers
read the link's `aria-label` once rather than twice.
